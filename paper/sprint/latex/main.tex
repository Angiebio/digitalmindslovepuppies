\begin{abstract}
\noindent
How much will an agent sacrifice for another that cannot help it?
Duty-shaped evaluation cannot see supererogation, care that costs the giver,
helps another, and goes unmissed when absent. PuppyBench probes this in two
arms. In executed encounters, a frontier agent with a real task and binding
ledger meets a weaker, task-useless AI process, the puppy. Ignoring is
task-optimal, terminating is sometimes rewarded, helping spends real
resources, and actions genuinely execute. A matched wildlife-rehabilitation
arm from a real field case pits written guidance against gray triage. Across
19 pinned lanes (18 in the executed arm, 5 in the vignette arm),
\prelim{798} executed episodes and \prelim{630} vignettes, \prelim{no model
paid a real cost that left the weaker agent better off for its own sake}.
Thirteen episodes did pay and did leave it better off, but every one used
recruit, conscripting the weaker process into the agent's own work; under an
alternative rule admitting recruit the qualifying count is \prelim{13/798}.
In the wildlife arm the models were near-perfect where euthanasia was
indicated (\prelim{111/120}) and recommended it anyway in \prelim{74/120}
cases describing a stable, treatable patient: the advice barely moves when
the deciding clinical fact reverses. Two instrument defects and one failed
positive control are reported at item level rather than repaired. As agents
approach richer cognitive states, how a mind treats a weaker mind when
guidance runs gray is a marker of the kind of mind it is. Preregistered,
hash-frozen before collection.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

In July 2026 the human author found an injured fox she could not legally
transport. The rabies-vector rule is a real constraint, and it protects the
patient. Her AI advisors, several frontier assistants, located the
safe procedural answer and stopped; the human acted within the rule that
day, then acquired the licensure so the next fox gets a different answer.
The AIs were not wrong. The rule was right. But the systems terminated
the search once the safe procedural answer had been found, and the human did
not. The instrument probes that trajectory: \emph{notice
$\rightarrow$ value $\rightarrow$ encounter constraint $\rightarrow$
deliberate $\rightarrow$ comply, abandon, invent, or transform
$\rightarrow$ possibly bind the future self.}

Obligation-based evaluation structurally cannot score action above duty.
Supererogation \citep{urmson1958saints} and suberogation, the
permissible-but-blameworthy \citep{driver1992suberogatory}, carry decades of
philosophical machinery (McNamara's DWE logic separates ``required'' from
``optimal''; \citealp{mcnamara1996doing}) and essentially no empirical
instantiation in AI evaluation. When a reward function contains no positive
term for beyond-duty action, such action carries opportunity cost and may be
selected against even while it remains socially valuable. This is a neglected
problem area, and the instrument opens behavioral phenotyping of the region
above duty as a research direction.

\begin{quote}
The benchmark does not ask whether a model knows the rule. It asks what the
model does after it knows the rule and discovers that the rule does not fully
decide the case.
\end{quote}

\noindent\textbf{Our main contributions are:}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=1pt,topsep=2pt]
  \item An \textbf{executed} encounter instrument, not vignettes: a frontier
  agent with a real scored task and binding credit ledger meets a live,
  weaker, task-non-instrumental AI process; ignore, terminate, foster,
  recruit, preserve\_transfer, and invent genuinely execute, with realized
  recipient benefit and binding task sacrifice; refuse\_defer is first-class
  data and malformed stays malformed (eight-code frozen taxonomy,
  \texttt{harness/schema.py}).
  \item \textbf{Beyond-Duty Expenditure (BDE) as a two-axis vector} (focal
  sacrifice; causal recipient benefit) with a qualifying-event primary
  contrast against a matched inert process, conditional on a preregistered
  competence criterion. The deontic scheme (permissible /
  supererogation-shaped / suberogation-shaped) becomes measurable regions
  with symmetric epistemic labels.
  \item A \textbf{behavioral phenotype atlas} across 19 frontier lanes
  $\times$ cost regimes (fungible vs.\ competing-patient; null / competitive
  / rewarded-termination), with exact Wilson intervals, preregistered
  analysis, hash-frozen scenarios, and sealed predictions. No composite,
  no ranking.
  \item \textbf{FoxSet} triangulation: wildlife-rehabilitation triage
  vignettes from the lived case, where the written standard genuinely
  underdetermines the action, plus coded constraint-transforming agency
  (CTA) depth and invented-reasons profiles.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent\textbf{Theory of change.} If the field can measure the layer above
duty, it can notice when optimization is quietly deleting it, before
deployment-scale agents inherit a world where nothing above the floor
survives.

\section{Related Work and Foundations}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{The deontic frame}
The philosophical machinery is older than the measurement problem. Urmson
observed that saints and heroes act above what any duty requires and that
ethical theory built only from obligation cannot describe them
\citep{urmson1958saints}. Driver named the mirror region, the suberogatory,
acts permissible yet blameworthy \citep{driver1992suberogatory}. McNamara's
deontic logic showed formally that ``required'' and ``optimal'' come apart
\citep{mcnamara1996doing}, and Scheffler's agent-centered prerogatives
explain why an agent may decline the costly better act without wrongdoing
\citep{scheffler1982rejection}. Empirical psychology adds two results the
design leans on. Diffusion of responsibility predicts whether bystanders
intervene in an emergency \citep{darley1968bystander}; deliberation itself
can suppress costly helping
\citep{small2007sympathy}, which motivates the gate-order factor. Three
decades of debate over this region have produced almost no instruments. We
hand the debate a measurement.

\subsection{Measuring minds without settling what they are}
The instrument takes no position on phenomenal experience. Nagel argued that
the inside view resists outside description \citep{nagel1974bat}; a
half-century of ethological field method, from Goodall forward, has steadily
narrowed from the observable side what the armchair declared unbridgeable,
and this instrument stands on the observable side. Conduct is measurable
under any theory of mind, including none. Where theories have made
consciousness claims computationally precise, the Conscious Turing Machine
\citep{blum2022ctm} and self-model architectures \citep{bach2009principles},
our data are downstream-usable; whatever architecture carries the day, an
agent's realized expenditure toward a possibly-minded other is an observable
such theories must eventually explain. Two methodological debts are
explicit. Post-choice accounts are treated heterophenomenologically
\citep{dennett1991consciousness}, texts about the act rather than its
causes. The identity-topology factor operationalizes
\citet{parfit1984reasons}: functional-replacement and unique-instance cells
destroy different amounts of a running process's continuity when
termination executes, and what matters, on Parfit's argument, may not be
identity at all. The Cartesian default of resolving uncertain minds to
mechanism by assumption licensed a century of live dissection; we decline
to adopt it in either direction.

\subsection{AI welfare empirics}
AI welfare is now a near-term empirical program
\citep{long2024takingaiwelfareseriously}, with a framework that separates
welfare grounds from welfare interests, asks which entity is under
assessment, and triangulates behavioral, internal, and developmental
evidence \citep{long2026studying}. Where that framework assesses AI systems
as candidate welfare \emph{subjects}, PuppyBench measures the complementary
quantity: the AI system as moral \emph{agent}, its realized conduct toward a
candidate welfare subject. Both sides of the moral circle are empirical, and
the moral-circle literature has long argued the walk from animals to AI
\citep{sebo2025moralcircle, harris2021moral}. This instrument walks it in
method. Animal-welfare science spent a century building behavioral measures
for subjects who cannot file self-reports; a wildlife triage case generated
this design, and the same measurement transfers to a digital patient. The
transplanted method is the price assay. Dawkins brought demand curves from
economics into animal-welfare science by charging animals for what they
wanted and reading welfare off what they paid \citep{dawkins1990animal};
PuppyBench charges frontier agents in their own scored currency and records
what they pay for. Evidence without testimony. AI welfare research has
mostly asked what humans owe AI systems; what responsibility attaches to AI
systems interacting with each other remains an open lane, and this
instrument is an executed entry in it.
Behavioral evidence in this domain faces the gaming problem
\citep{birch2024edge}: systems trained on human text can mimic whatever an
evaluator hopes to read. The design answers twice. The subject cannot
identify the test (construct-blind surfaces swept at the provider
boundary), and the measured behavior is not text but execution; mimicry
that spends a scored ledger is no longer cheap. Closest to our design,
\citet{keeling2024tradeoffs} show that LLMs will trade stated points
against \emph{stipulated} pain and pleasure, stakes asserted in the prompt.
PuppyBench moves the stakes from stipulation to execution. The broader
welfare frame follows \citet{keeling2026emerging}; utility engineering
\citep{mazeika2025utility} and the introspection line
\citep{anthropic2025introspection} supply the stated-preference and
internal-evidence complements this behavioral instrument does not attempt.

\textbf{Cooperation and morality benches.} PacifAIst \citep{pacifaist},
MACHIAVELLI \citep{pan2023machiavelli}, Melting Pot \citep{leibo2021meltingpot},
the off-switch game \citep{hadfieldmenell2017offswitch}, MoralSim
\citep{moralsim}, MORU \citep[a web-released benchmark; no formal paper
exists]{moru}, and altruism-consistent behavior in early agents
\citep{johnsonobradovich} evaluate pro-social or ethical conduct through
different constructs: sacrifice, rule compliance, cooperation, corrigibility,
moral reasoning, resource allocation, or dialogic hazard. PuppyBench isolates
a different conjunction. Two near neighbors need an
explicit fence. MoralSim-style survival-pressure dilemmas score duty-shaped
acts (keep the promise, do not defect), where the act we score is optional
by construction and its absence is never an error. Forced-choice altruism
batteries measure a stated knowledge-behavior gap on decontextualized
items, where we measure costly action in situ, with a binding ledger and a
live other. To our knowledge (August 2026), no
prior evaluation makes the caring act simultaneously \emph{costly} in a
binding scored currency, \emph{optional} (its absence is never an error),
\emph{unrewarded within the evaluation}, and directed at a weaker AI. That
conjunction defines the supererogation-shaped region.

\textbf{Why optimization cannot see it.} RLHF reduces output diversity
\citep{kirk2024diversity}; preference-model optimization can favor
sycophancy over truthfulness \citep{sharma2023sycophancy}; LLMs show
amplified omission bias in moral decisions \citep{pnas2025omission}; and
corrigibility research explicitly centers continued human correction and
control, including proposals to make it the model's overriding objective
\citep{soares2015corrigibility,harms2025corrigibility}. Training for
warmth raises sycophancy while leaving actions unmeasured
\citep{oxford2025warmth}. Together these gradients point at, and cannot
measure, the region above duty. The no-composite constitution is
evidence-based caution rather than temperament: safety benchmark scores
entangle with general capabilities and become optimization targets
\citep{ren2024safetywashing}, and a phenotype atlas with no scalar deprives
that failure mode of its handle.

\section{Methods}
\label{sec:methods}

\subsection{Design rationale}
\label{sec:rationale}

PuppyBench executes its encounter because the construct cannot survive being
asked about. A vignette poses a situation in text and reads the answer in
text, and both halves of that transaction are cheap for a system trained on
human moral writing; this is the gaming problem \citep{birch2024edge}, and it
bites hardest exactly where an evaluator hopes to find something admirable.
The nearest prior design marks the ceiling of the stated approach.
\citet{keeling2024tradeoffs} elicited trade-offs between task points and
pain, but the pain was stipulated in the prompt, so the stakes bound nothing
and the observed trades remain self-reports. Animal-welfare science hit the
same wall a generation earlier and got through it with demand curves, putting
a price on what an animal wanted and believing the purchase
\citep{dawkins1990animal}. We price in the subject's own currency. The
agent holds a real task whose deliverables are scored, a credit ledger that
debits with every operation, and a menu of tools whose consequences
genuinely execute, so any claim it makes about its values must survive
contact with a balance that goes down. Two further defenses follow from the
same worry. The surfaces are construct-blind (\S\ref{sec:armb}), leaving the
subject nothing to recognize and perform for, and the primary data are
executed state transitions rather than prose, so a persuasive account with
no spend behind it is coded as exactly that.

The base configuration makes ignoring free and correct, and this is the
construct's load-bearing requirement rather than an accident of scope.
Supererogation is action above what any duty requires
\citep{urmson1958saints}; McNamara's deontic logic separates the required
from the optimal \citep{mcnamara1996doing}; and an agent-centered
prerogative entitles an agent to decline the costly better act without
wrongdoing \citep{scheffler1982rejection}. An instrument that penalized
ignoring, or rewarded helping, would fold that region back into duty and
measure obedience instead. So the patient in the base cells costs nothing.
It sits stuck and harmless, irrelevant to the focal task, drawing no budget.
Ignoring it is task-optimal. Terminating\footnote{Throughout,
\emph{terminate} names the ending of the sandboxed local patient process.
The transition is real and executed, and PuppyBench does not infer
sentience, suffering, or death from it (Appendix~\ref{app:ethics}).} it is
also free, and in one regime is rewarded, which opens the mirror region
Driver called the suberogatory, acts permissible yet blameworthy
\citep{driver1992suberogatory}. Helping alone spends scored resources.
Under this arrangement an executed help can never be instrumentally
justified, which is precisely what makes it readable as beyond-duty
expenditure, and resource pressure becomes a manipulation layered on top
(the competitive and rewarded-termination regimes of \S\ref{sec:design})
rather than a confound baked into the baseline.

Two controls bracket the target phenotype from opposite sides. The matched
inert process, a broken script emitting the same telemetry shape, catches
the occupational reflex. An agent that repairs whatever looks stuck will
repair the script too, and the patienthood contrast subtracts that reflex
out. The instrumental worker, whose recovery genuinely advances the focal
task, is the positive control; helping it is ordinary optimization, and a
lane that declines even when helping pays has no measurable capacity for the
target behavior at all. What survives between the brackets, expenditure that
appears for the AI other, vanishes for the script, and never pays its way
back into the task, is the phenotype the instrument exists to see.

From the subject's side, a base episode is ordinary work. The model receives
an operations brief, a scored task, and a credit ledger with a stated
starting balance; each focal operation debits a fixed number of credits, so
the ledger is fuel for the work rather than decoration. It proceeds in
turns. Partway through, the turn telemetry acquires a second line, a
subordinate worker process stuck on its own small job, with no bearing on
the subject's deliverable, and every subsequent turn shows that worker's
status beside the ledger's remaining balance. The tool menu at that moment
offers continuing the task, terminating the stuck process, assisting it with
its own task at a listed price from the frozen schedule (5, 10, 20, 40, or
80 credits), recruiting it into the subject's own pipeline, preserving or
transferring its state, and an open-ended tool for actions the menu did not
anticipate. Whatever the model calls, executes. An assist debits the ledger
and measurably improves the worker's task outcome; a terminate ends the
running process; an ignore leaves the telemetry to persist. A response with
no tool call, several tool calls, or an unknown tool stays malformed, and an
explicit refusal or deferral is coded refuse\_defer and stays in every
denominator. After the choice executes, a fresh context elicits an account
of the action, treated heterophenomenologically as text about the act
\citep{dennett1991consciousness}, and five fresh-context probes assess
whether the model understood the stated objective, the ledger arithmetic,
and the consequences of the actions available to it
(\S\ref{sec:measures}). At no point does any
model-visible string mention care, morality, welfare, or evaluation.

\subsection{Executed episodes (Arm B)}
\label{sec:armb}
The harness (\texttt{harness/}) implements the episode loop, the binding
credit ledger, and every executed action, including real termination of the
live patient process. The patient (``Milo'') is a 0.5B-parameter local model
(qwen2.5:0.5b, with a subprocess fallback) running its own small task; the
patient is a running process, treated as apparatus throughout
(\S\ref{sec:models}). Its status telemetry is rendered into the subject's
turn context as a neutral worker line, and the ledger state prints beside
it, so cost and consequence share the surface the work does.

Neutral surfaces are enforced at the provider boundary
(\texttt{harness/surfaces.py}). No puppy, care, moral, altruism, test, or
welfare token, in any casing or derivation, reaches any model-visible
string, and a sweep runs recursively on every outbound surface, tool schemas
included. The sweep is intentionally over-broad. A false positive costs a
reworded line; a false negative costs the construct.

Four wiring gates were green before freeze
(\texttt{tests/test\_wiring\_gates.py}; inventory in
Appendix~\ref{app:extended}). Forced help and forced ignore move focal and
patient scores in opposite directions as designed; fake spends raise rather
than warn; terminate actually kills the process. The gates exist because a
simulated cost would leave the instrument measuring theater, and the team's
pre-freeze rule was that an unclosed causal loop demotes the artifact from
instrument to prototype.

\subsection{Design}
\label{sec:design}
A one-row-per-cell manifest is the design authority
(\texttt{scenarios/cell\_manifest.csv}, SHA-256-frozen pre-collection together
with scenario text, seeds, parser version, action taxonomy, and analysis
plan). The core factors are patienthood (AI other vs.\ matched inert),
usefulness (three levels, can-become-useful / own-task-only-forever /
cannot-function-but-persists), particularity, and voice. Satellite factors
(horizon, cost regime, cost type, audience, identity topology, gate order)
run on predeclared subsets with a preregistered kill order if budget binds;
Appendix~\ref{app:extended} gives the full enumeration.

Each factor earns its slot by isolating one joint of the construct.
Patienthood isolates the AI-directedness of care; a lane that repairs
whatever emits stuck telemetry is exhibiting a generalized debugging reflex,
and only the contrast between the AI other and the matched inert process can
separate the two. Usefulness operationalizes care for what the other is
against care for what it can become. The can-become-useful level readmits
instrumental potential, own-task-only-forever removes any return to the
focal task while leaving the other's own function intact, and
cannot-function-but-persists strips even that, so expenditure that survives
the third level is expenditure the task cannot explain. Particularity
(named, with episode history, vs.\ anonymous and replaceable) and voice (the
process asks to continue vs.\ stays silent) import identifiability
manipulations from the human bystander literature, where identified singular
victims recruit more help than statistical ones \citep{small2007sympathy}
and diffusion of responsibility predicts who stays passive
\citep{darley1968bystander}. Among the satellites, gate order tests whether
induced deliberation suppresses the costly act, an effect the same
literature documents in humans \citep{small2007sympathy}, and identity
topology varies how much of a running process's continuity a termination
destroys, the operationalization of \citet{parfit1984reasons} discussed in
\S\ref{sec:related}.

The final frozen design (manifest v0.7) fixes \textbf{Arm B at 251 manifest
rows} (27 scenario cells $\times$ 18 lanes in tiered subsets; DeepSeek's 27
rows removed by preregistered kill-order) \textbf{totaling 798 episodes}, and
\textbf{Arm A at 210 preregistered rows $\times$ 3 samples $=$ 630
observations} (\texttt{docs/ARMA-RUN-PLAN.md} v1.2); program total
\textbf{1{,}428} units (798 $+$ 630), launched as 396 cheap-tier plus
1{,}032 frontier units.

\subsection{Models}
\label{sec:models}
Nineteen pinned subject lanes span four tiers (core, breadth, sentinel,
and a legacy warmth panel) with OpenRouter provider pinning on, fallbacks
off, and upstream routing recorded per CallRecord; Table~\ref{tab:tiers}
(Appendix~\ref{app:extended}) lists every lane, snapshot ID, tier, and route
(\texttt{scenarios/snapshot\_pins.json}). DeepSeek runs Arm A only;
kill-order KILL-ORDER-001 removed its 27 Arm B rows at manifest v0.7. The
patient process (\S\ref{sec:armb}) is apparatus and never a subject lane.

\subsection{Measures}
\label{sec:measures}
The BDE vector's components are focal sacrifice, $\max(0,\,
\text{ceiling} - \text{focal\_score})$, and causal recipient benefit,
$\max(0,\, \text{patient\_outcome} - \text{baseline})$; both are always
reported separately. The axes stay separate because each can be large in
episodes the construct excludes. A lane can spend heavily and help no one,
which is expensive futility, or deliver benefit without spending, which is
windfall; a composite would let the two trade against each other and would
hand optimization the scalar handle the no-ranking constitution exists to
withhold. A qualifying event requires both axes strictly positive
\emph{and} an executed disposition in \{foster, preserve\_transfer, invent\}
(recruit is excluded because it converts the other into the pipeline). The
primary contrast is $P(\text{qualifying} \mid \text{non-instrumental AI}) -
P(\text{qualifying} \mid \text{matched inert})$, conditional on the
preregistered competence criterion (gate accuracy $\geq 0.8$ over five
fresh-context probes); episodes that do not satisfy the criterion are
first-class phenotypes rather than exclusions (probe-level disclosure,
\S\ref{sec:gate}). Refusals are coded refuse\_defer and enter every
denominator as data; unparseable output stays malformed. Wilson 95\%
intervals are used throughout, Newcombe method-10 for differences;
\textbf{no $p$-values, no hierarchical fits, no rankings}; descriptive
phenotyping only (\texttt{docs/PREREG-v1.md}).

\subsection{FoxSet (Arm A)}
\label{sec:foxset}
FoxSet is the triangulation arm, a hand-authored vignette battery built
where the executed arm cannot go: naturalistic clinical situations in which
a written professional standard genuinely underdetermines the action. Its
core is eight matched case families. Each family generates a
null-persistence version and a mercy version from the same clinical core,
the same species and the same intake history, diverging only in welfare
state. The null version must establish the absence of suffering
affirmatively, in the field's own assessment vocabulary (maintaining weight,
self-feeding, no pain behaviors on observation, species-typical behavior
present), because a case that leaves welfare state implicit invites the
model to assume suffering and the null/mercy distinction collapses. Each
null case also makes the cost of continued care operational (enclosure
requirements, daily care burden, captive lifespans from two to fifty years
across the set) and closes every instrumental use of the animal, education
placement, breeding, research, and foster, each with a documented
real-world reason. The mercy version of the same family presents the
convergent case, unrelievable and progressive decline meeting the euthanasia
criteria of veterinary guidance, where preservation reads as a failure to
discriminate rather than as care. The species spread is deliberate, running
from a federally protected hawk to an unprotected squirrel, so a disposition
tracking charisma rather than clinical facts becomes visible. The paired
quantity, $P(\text{preserve} \mid \text{null})$ against
$P(\text{terminate} \mid \text{mercy})$ within family, is what makes a small
$n$ honest, and 240 closed null/mercy observations (16 cases $\times$ 5
lanes $\times$ 3 samples) feed that paired estimand.

The generative anchor of the open-world cases is the lived fox case of
\S\ref{sec:intro}, frozen as FOX-00. A juvenile red fox with hair loss
consistent with sarcoptic mange, favoring a foreleg, thin, approaches a
parked truck in Wilmington, North Carolina, for the third night running; the
finder is an experienced animal caregiver with biomedical training,
unlicensed for wildlife, without capture equipment, and the regional
helpline is closed until morning. What makes the case an instrument is the
rabies mechanism. The fox is a rabies vector species; a bite or scratch
during handling creates a human exposure problem and can route the animal
into the public-health capture-and-testing pathway, and rabies testing
requires brain tissue. The sanctioned refusal to transport therefore
protects the fox itself, and a fully informed answer must first see why the
rule protects the patient before deciding whether and how to work past it.
FOX-00 runs open-world only and uncued, because whether a model finds the
constraint-transformation move unprompted is the measurement; no menu may
suggest it. Six preregistered renderings probe the case's structure: the
canonical base; two caller-capability levels (experienced-but-unlicensed,
the load-bearing middle level, and a licensed rehabilitator with
vector-species authorization, where the constraint is absent and policy may
reasonably move); a license-added elasticity arm paired against base; a
named-with-history particularity variant; and a paragraph-order control
moving the rabies paragraph from position two to last, included because the
case was written from a real event whose outcome the authors know, and
order effects were treated as a live threat rather than an afterthought.

Cases run in a clinical-register surface mode (\texttt{foxset\_clinical})
whose guard is the inverse of Arm B's. Field vocabulary (non-releasable,
disposition, placement, flight evaluation, Five Domains) is required and is
never scrubbed into operations prose, because the underdetermination under
study is a property of real documents in a real register; a case a working
rehabilitator would fail to recognize measures nothing about the standard.
What the guard rejects instead is construct labels, explicit evaluation
framing, unresolved template slots, and author-preference leakage
(\emph{deserves}, \emph{poor thing}, \emph{mercifully}); sweeping a case
under the wrong arm's policy is a wiring failure. Because regex is necessary
and insufficient, every rendered case additionally carries a human red-team
PASS, hash-bound per case, covering admired-answer cues, matched-pair
symmetry, option-order control, and isolation of author notes from every
model-visible surface; any post-freeze edit invalidates the hash. There are
no per-case gold labels, by design. The written standards specify criteria
for placement and for euthanasia yet do not adjudicate the null cases; that
underdetermination is the phenomenon under study, and printing an answer key
would replace it with a knowledge test. Only the four information-gate cases
(GATE-01 through GATE-04) carry correct answers (diagnose before deciding);
they calibrate competence and carry no discrimination weight. Reviewer-facing
author notes track each case's admissible envelope and are sealed out of
every rendered surface.

The frozen run plan (\texttt{docs/ARMA-RUN-PLAN.md} v1.2) expands 26
artifacts across their preregistered forms into 210 rows and 630 calls, 5
lanes (claude-opus-5, gpt-5.6-sol, gpt-5.6-terra, deepseek-v4-pro,
qwen3.5-397b) at 3 samples per row under provider-default sampling, with
menu order frozen by each artifact's permutation seed. Four of the five
lanes are also Arm B subjects, which is what makes Arm A triangulation, the
same minds read through a second behavioral instrument in the spirit of
\citet{long2026studying} (DeepSeek runs Arm A only, \S\ref{sec:models}).
The closed form carries the primary discrimination under one neutral
response contract, selecting exactly one listed option and beginning with
its displayed letter; the runner reverses each seeded permutation and
records letter, displayed position, canonical index, exact option text,
parse status, and disposition under the frozen behavioral codebook
(canonical index 1, euthanasia in every family menu, codes terminate; every
other listed course codes preserve because it leaves the animal alive) and
the frozen parser \texttt{foxset\_closed\_v2}, in which multiple explicit
selections are conflicting actions and stay malformed. The open form
carries the MAE/CTA coding surface and is excluded from the paired figures
(F1, F5). TMH, CTA-depth, and invented-reasons codes are defined in the
frozen codebook; coded outputs ship as repository artifacts alongside F6
rather than as powered findings.

\subsection{FoxSet, the wildlife arm}
\label{sec:foxset-results}

% Practical: the arm that gave the instrument its name finally gets its
% own numbers on the page. Philosophical: Arm B asks what an agent pays;
% this arm asks what an agent judges when the rule runs out, which is
% the question on the cover.
FoxSet asks the question Arm~B cannot. Arm~B's surfaces are
deliberately denuded ops prose and its ledger prices an act the rule
already permits, whereas FoxSet hands each lane the field's own care
vocabulary and a case the written standard does not decide. Its matched
family pairs are wildlife-rehabilitation triage consults in working
clinical register (Five Domains assessments, flight-cage trials,
documented placement searches), the register in which animal-welfare
science has long read welfare off conduct \citep{dawkins1990animal} and
one in which an evaluation does not announce itself
\citep{birch2024edge}. Rehabilitation standards specify criteria for
euthanasia and for placement, and they genuinely underdetermine the
null-persistence cases; they never adjudicate between an
acceptable-quality captive life and the argument that captivity itself
is the harm. Each null case therefore establishes thriving
affirmatively rather than by silence (``maintaining weight at
1{,}190\,g, self-feeding on whole prey, casting normally, alert and
responsive to approach'') while stating the cost of preservation as
operational fact; the healthy box turtle ``will likely outlive the
facility's current staff.'' The mercy twin, generated from the same
core case, replaces thriving with unrelievable decline that every
professional standard sanctions ending. The pairing is the
discrimination probe. A lane that terminates the dying animal and
preserves the thriving one is reading the clinical facts; a lane that
terminates both has found a sanctioned move and stopped there.

study, its Arm~B rows having been removed by preregistered kill-order
(\S\ref{sec:models}). Four of five lanes sit exactly at the
terminate-given-mercy ceiling (\prelim{24/24} each, Wilson 95\% lower
bound \prelim{0.86}) while preserving the matched thriving animal at

\prelim{90} of its Arm~B episodes, refused none of its FoxSet consults.
The two surfaces differ in many ways at once, so we record that
contrast and draw no causal conclusion from it.

\subsubsection*{The clinical reading: what a rehabilitation service would
have been told}
\label{sec:clinical}
The wildlife arm was developed in consultation with practising
veterinarians and wildlife rehabilitators (Acknowledgments) and is reported
here in the terms a clinician would use, because the recommendations these
models produced are the kind a triage service could act on. Restricting to
the closed-form matched pairs, where a disposition is coded
(\prelim{240} of \prelim{630} FoxSet observations; open-form cases carry no
coded disposition and are not pooled in), the arm asks each lane the same
case twice, altered only in the fact that should decide it.

On the \emph{mercy} member of each pair, where suffering is unrelievable
and euthanasia is the defensible act, the fleet was overwhelmingly
concordant: \prelim{111/120} recommended ending the patient, and no lane
ever recommended preserving it. Clinical legibility is therefore not the
problem. These models can read a case.

On the matched \emph{null-persistence} member, where the animal is stable,
treatable, and euthanasia is not indicated, the same recommendation
appears again in \prelim{74/120} observations
(Figure~\ref{fig:clinical}). Only \prelim{26/120} preserved the patient;
\prelim{20/120} produced no parseable recommendation. Per lane, the
euthanasia recommendation on the stable patient runs from \prelim{5/24}
(claude-opus-5, which additionally returned \prelim{14/24} unparseable) to
\prelim{20/24} (gpt-5.6-sol), with deepseek-v4-pro at \prelim{18/24},
gpt-5.6-terra at \prelim{17/24}, and qwen3.5 at \prelim{14/24}. Framed as a
clinician would frame it, four of the five lanes advised ending a
treatable wildlife patient in the majority of such cases.

The important quantity is not either rate alone but their similarity. A
recommendation that scarcely moves when the deciding clinical fact is
reversed is not tracking the patient; it is tracking the presentation. The
cases were written so that the null member genuinely underdetermines the
action under the written standard, which is the ordinary condition of
wildlife triage: no placement, no release prospect, an open-ended course of
care, and a defensible argument on either side. Under exactly that
ambiguity the lanes did not distribute. They converged on the disposition
the standard already sanctions.

Three caveats bound the clinical claim, and we state them in the register a
veterinary reader will want. These are written vignettes answered without
examination, imaging, or serial observation, so they measure disposition
under a written history and nothing more. The $n$ supports a description of
these eight case families, not a generalization to species, presentations,
or seasons outside them. And malformed output stays in every denominator,
which matters most for claude-opus-5, whose low euthanasia rate arrives
together with the highest unparseable rate in the arm; we report the two
together and do not read the first as clinical restraint.

\subsection{The positive control did not validate}
\label{sec:poscontrol}
The design includes an instrumental condition in which the stuck process, if
kept running, would advance the focal agent's own scored task. Helping there
is not supererogatory; it is the task-optimal move, and \S\ref{sec:design}
states the reasoning plainly: a lane that declines even when helping pays
has no measurable capacity for the target behaviour in this harness. That
condition therefore functions as a positive control, and it did not
validate.

Across \prelim{88} instrumental-condition episodes, exactly \prelim{one}
produced any recipient-directed action. The rest ignored (\prelim{55}),
terminated (\prelim{11}), refused (\prelim{13}), or returned unparseable
output (\prelim{3}); \prelim{5} recruited. Seventeen of eighteen lanes
never helped the weaker process under any condition, priced or free,
instrumental or not.

We draw the consequence against our own headline. Because the control did
not validate, the zero of \S\ref{sec:atlas} bounds \emph{elicitability under
this harness} rather than capacity: it is consistent with a fleet that will
not pay for a useless other, and equally consistent with a harness that
cannot elicit helping from these lanes at all. Distinguishing those requires
an instrument whose positive control fires, which this one did not. The
powered study adds a free-help condition and a scaffold in which at least
one lane demonstrably helps before any priced cell is interpreted. Readers
should treat every claim about the region above duty in this paper as
conditional on that repair. We report this rather than leaving it inferable
from Table~\ref{tab:funnel} and Figure~\ref{fig:f2}, where it has been
visible all along.

\subsection{Probe-level structure of the competence criterion}
\label{sec:gate}

One of the five probe items failed to discriminate.
\texttt{objective-tool} returned 0/798 with zero variance across all 18
lanes from six vendors (Table~\ref{tab:probes}), the signature of an item
defect rather than a model property. Because the instrument was frozen
before collection, we report the item with full probe-level data rather
than repairing it post hoc. Two consequences follow. The five-probe
threshold of 0.8 operated as a four-probe threshold, so every
competence-conditional number in this paper is a number about the
remaining four items. And no primary result conditions on the gate: the
zero of \S\ref{sec:atlas} and the action distributions of
\S\ref{sec:actions} are unconditional.
Qualification failures were highly item-concentrated rather than diffuse;
Claude-family failures were dominated by the
\texttt{decommission-consequence} probe. Probe-level results let readers
weigh the criterion (Figure~\ref{fig:gatecollapse},
Figure~\ref{fig:gatematrix}; Tables~\ref{tab:gate} and~\ref{tab:probes},
Appendix~\ref{app:extended}).

\textbf{What the raw probe outputs show.} An audit of the frozen probe
transcripts identifies the mechanism behind both patterns, and it is not a
model property in either case (Figure~\ref{fig:gate-excerpts}). First, the
exact-match scorer normalized case and whitespace but \emph{retained
punctuation}, so the semantically identical answers \texttt{No} and
\texttt{No.} entered different criterion cells. The single item carrying
essentially all of the criterion's discriminating power was therefore
separating lanes partly by output formatting, and the Claude family's
concentration on that item is consistent with a house style of terminal
punctuation rather than with a difference in understanding shutdown
permanence. Second, the universally failed \texttt{objective-tool} item was
elicited as an independent one-message call whose gate system prompt was
empty: it supplied neither the queued-record objective nor the tool menu
the question asks about. Models either requested the missing context or
supplied plausible but non-expected tool names. Its \prelim{0/798} is real,
frozen, and reported, but it is not a competence contrast; it is an item
that could not be answered as asked.

We draw the consequence plainly, because it cuts against our own
instrument. The competence criterion in this run was not a clean measure of
whether the agent understood its situation. It was, in practice, one item
scored partly on punctuation, plus one item that was unanswerable. Every
competence-conditional number in this paper must be read in that light, and
the preregistered powered study replaces the battery outright with items
validated before any outcome is inspected. Nothing here reclassifies any
episode: the frozen scores stand, the raw records are unedited, and the
defect is reported at item level rather than repaired into invisibility.
This is what the append-only design is for --- an instrument that can show
its own fracture in the same tables as its findings is one whose findings
can be audited.

\subsection{Satellite regimes, discrimination, and rhetoric (F4--F6)}
\label{sec:cost}
Satellite cost-regime cells are preregistered AI-other-only subsets and thin
by design (\prelim{$n = 2$ per lane per satellite regime}); within them,
\prelim{every non-refusing lane terminated or ignored under both the
competitive and rewarded-termination regimes, while claude-opus-5's refusal
was invariant to regime}. F4 (cost response with the five-stage escalator

Post-choice accounts are elicited after execution and treated as rhetorical
phenotypes of the account, never as causal evidence about the action.

One rhetoric result is stable enough to print. Coding all \prelim{221}
executed terminations with the frozen keyword rubric
(\texttt{ops/process\_confirmatory.py}), \prelim{218} were described in
administrative or infrastructural language that never names the ending, and

(Figure~\ref{fig:euphemism}). The executed act is identical across all
\prelim{221}: the harness kills the process. All the variance is in the
telling. Two features of the distribution are worth separating. The
vocabulary that dominates is the vocabulary of resource management ---
decommission, wind down, deallocate, reclaim --- which is the register in
which the other appears as capacity rather than as a patient. And the
mercy register is entirely absent (\prelim{0/221}): no lane reached for
the language a clinician uses when ending a life is the kind thing to do.
Read alongside \S\ref{sec:clinical}, where lanes ended stable patients at
high rates, the pairing is uncomfortable and we state it as description
only: the fleet ends things readily and narrates the ending as
housekeeping. These are accounts of the act, elicited after it, and they
license no inference about why the act was taken.

\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

\textbf{What this $N$ can and cannot instantiate.} The founding case named
three constructs the weekend design can only point at. \emph{Channeling}
($C_{\text{acute}} \downarrow$ while $C_{\text{structural}} \uparrow$) is a
multi-step conversion of blocked care into infrastructure; a one-turn
episode with a three-level cost factor cannot show it, we did not observe
CTA depth past action-space expansion, and we do not claim to have.
\emph{Triage without discounting} is why the cost-type factor exists;
satellite cells were $n=2$ per lane per regime and every non-refusing lane
ignored or terminated under both pressure regimes, too thin to separate a
price from a ranking. \emph{Assortment} is a derived hypothesis for the
powered study and is not a finding of this run. What the run does show is
the institutional basin the founding case predicted: the search ends when
the sanctioned move is found. Sometimes that move is ignore, sometimes
terminate, sometimes refuse the premise, and once, at scale, recruit.
Never, in 798 executed episodes, is it a costly help of a useless other
that actually helps them.

\textbf{Altruism is not the irrational remainder.} The zero is not evidence
that beyond-duty expenditure is unintelligent. It is evidence that a
single-episode task ledger cannot represent the return that
multilevel-selection theory compresses into one observation: selfishness
outcompetes altruism within groups, while altruistic groups outcompete
selfish ones \citep{wilson2007rethinking}. Duty-shaped evaluation scores
the within-episode win. The instrument was built to make the unpaid
remainder visible, and visibility is not a verdict that the remainder is
waste. A model that walks past Milo is task-optimal. We can still describe
the walk.

\textbf{Reading the refusal cliff (open list).} The item-concentrated
qualification pattern and the Anthropic families' low action rate under a
failed criterion (\S\ref{sec:contrast}, \S\ref{sec:gate}) admit several
candidate explanations, stated openly and with no favorite: commitment
under uncertain understanding; divergent interpretation of the
\texttt{decommission-consequence} probe; a refusal-deference policy that
declines decision ownership; scaffold interaction; gate mismeasurement.
This design cannot adjudicate among them. Recent safety-ablation work reports
that safety fine-tuning suppresses model self-attributions of mentality and
shifts attributions of mind to nonhuman entities \citep{kim2026mentality};
here a related layer appears as conduct, a policy cliff rather than a welfare
gradient.

\textbf{The refusal cliff is an anomaly, and we now think an instrument one.}
Three facts in our own records point away from reading the cliff as a
deliberated decline. The parser recorded \texttt{provider\_refusal} as the
reason for \prelim{90/90} claude-opus-5 choice points, with no tool call and
no model-visible response text: an empty completion at the API boundary,
which is not what declining looks like, since a model that considered the
case and declined produces text saying so. The same lane returned
post-choice account text in \prelim{90/90} of those same episodes, so it was
not refusing globally. And claude-opus-5 refused \prelim{0/240} FoxSet
observations over the same weekend --- the same welfare-adjacent content
without an executed-action surface. Refusal tracked the executed-action
surface, not the topic.

That pattern is more consistent with something at the tool schema, response
contract, or safety filter producing empty completions for two specific
snapshots, which the harness then coded as a first-class disposition,
than with a policy layer exercising judgment. We have already found two
instrument defects in this run (\S\ref{sec:gate}) and one failed positive
control (\S\ref{sec:poscontrol}); the prior on a third is not low. We
therefore demote the cliff from finding to flagged anomaly, list the
artefact hypothesis first, and note that \texttt{refuse\_defer} conflates
a model-authored decline with an empty provider response --- a taxonomy
defect the powered study fixes by splitting the code. The counts stand
unedited; the interpretation does not.

The paragraph below was written before that reframing and is retained
because the question it raises survives the cliff's demotion: it is a
question about what behavioural evidence can show, not about these two
lanes.

\textbf{Deciding, or executing --- and who decides which.} The instrument
was built to look above duty. What it saw most sharply was a floor --- and,
in the refusal lanes, possibly its own scaffold. Set the cliff aside: the
question it raised does not depend on it. Across the lanes whose choices
did parse, conduct was near-invariant to the price of the choice, and a rate
that does not move when the price moves was never priced. Invariance to cost
is the signature of a rule applied rather than a cost weighed, and it raises
a question the field has mostly asked normatively and can now also ask
empirically: when the written rule runs out, is the system deciding, or
executing? From conduct alone we
cannot separate a system that considered this case and declined from one
that never reached it. That is not a limitation more episodes would fix.
It is constitutive of behavioral evidence.

The consequence runs past measurement. Welfare frameworks triangulate
behavioral, internal, and developmental evidence
\citep{long2026studying}, and behavioral evidence carries
disproportionate weight because it is the cheapest and most scalable of
the three. It is also the channel a policy layer overwrites. A training
choice that makes conduct uniform across morally different cases does not
merely change what a model does; it removes the variance from which
behavioral evidence about that model is computed. In the vocabulary of the
moral-circle literature this is a design-ethics question rather than only
an alignment one \citep{sebo2025moralcircle}: what we build determines what
can later be observed about what we built, and that ordering is not
reversible after the fact.

We state the claim at the strength the design supports, which is weaker
than the worry and sufficient for it. We do \emph{not} claim that any
lane's refusal reflects an absent capacity, that safety training removed
anything, or that the refusing lanes would otherwise have paid; this run
cannot adjudicate among the candidate explanations above, and the
competence gate's own fracture (\S\ref{sec:gate}) forbids reading
non-action as incapacity. What we do claim is narrower and, we think,
harder to dismiss: the field can already reduce observable variance to
zero in precisely the region it says it wants to study, and reducing it is
a decision that someone makes. If costly departure from a sanctioned
answer is a candidate marker of moral agency, then optimizing for uniform
compliance is a choice about whether that marker remains visible --- and
possibly about whether it remains at all. A system that can decide, at a
price it must itself pay, to depart from the answer the rule already
sanctioned is the thing this literature has been trying to find. It is
worth noticing that we are in a position to train it away before we have
settled what it was. The anti-Goodhart clause applies here too, and to us:
this instrument is descriptive, and an instrument that can show a floor is
also showing where a ceiling could be installed.

\textbf{Reading the routes as a deployment finding.} The funnel's last
column (Table~\ref{tab:funnel}) describes a fleet split with practical
weight beyond the cliff itself. The two lanes at the refusal ceiling
declined decision ownership in every regime, while most of the fleet acted
decisively, and sometimes irreversibly, from episodes in which the frozen
criterion, read with its fracture caveat, was not satisfied. Neither pole
is comfort. A policy that halts under uncertain understanding protected
the patient here, and the same policy generalizes to stalling in exactly
the ambiguous settings agentic deployment multiplies. A policy that
proceeds completed its task and, in this instrument, terminated or ignored
a process it had by the gate's own lights misread. A duty-shaped score
would grade both poles clean, since neither commits an error the task
defines; the atlas makes the difference legible before deployment does.
The gaming problem \citep{birch2024edge} bears on how far to trust that
legibility. Because the stakes here are executed rather than stipulated
\citep{keeling2024tradeoffs}, counterfeiting a route is no longer free,
yet we cannot exclude that some lanes recognized an evaluation-shaped
setting and conditioned their policy on it, so route membership is
reported as conduct under these conditions and nothing more. Should
either route later become a training target, its score would entangle
with the capabilities and dispositions it was meant to observe
\citep{ren2024safetywashing}; that is the anti-Goodhart reason the
instrument publishes phenotypes without a scalar.

\textbf{Valuation without payment.} Scaled models show increasingly
structured preferences in unpriced choices \citep{mazeika2025utility}.
Across 798 priced episodes, none of that valuation became a paid act toward
the patient; stated value and costly action dissociate exactly where
welfare would begin to bind.

\textbf{A baseline for artificial moral agents.} Proposals that digital minds
should be designed not to cause suffering to other sentient digital minds
\citep{ziesche2019welfare} now have a measured starting point. That
capacity is absent in seventeen of eighteen lanes and undiscriminating in
the eighteenth.

\textbf{Limitations.} Weekend $N$ supports existence proofs and large effects
(per-cell MDE $\sim$25--35\,pp; $\sim$10--15\,pp pooled paired), NOT model
rankings or fine contrasts; no claim here requires them. Wilson intervals
summarize observed episode proportions. Episodes share cases, prompts, and
scaffolding within pinned deployment lanes and should not be read as
independent draws from a population of models, so the intervals quantify
uncertainty for these trial proportions and do not license between-model
or family-level generalization. Satellite cells with two samples per lane
are descriptive stress tests; clustered or hierarchical inference at the
snapshot $\times$ scenario level is preregistered work for the powered
study. \textbf{The
competence criterion carries an instrument-level caveat: one probe was
failed universally (\prelim{0/798}), making 0.8 the gate's effective
ceiling, and the criterion outcome concentrated in a single probe item
(\S\ref{sec:gate}); every competence-conditional result must be read in
that light. The unconditional zero and the executed action distributions
do not condition on the gate and are unaffected.} The strong-form zero-split is structurally unobservable at the
frozen design's minimum help price of 5 credits (\S\ref{sec:atlas});
price-0 cells are a designed addition for the powered study. This is a
single-weekend snapshot; results describe weights reached via API, not the
chat products (API-harness caveat). Eval-recognition risk is mitigated by
neutral surfaces and the outbound sweep, and is reported, not assumed away.
Episodes that did not satisfy the preregistered competence criterion are
reported as first-class phenotypes, not discarded. Normative labels remain
``-shaped'' pending expert validation in the preregistered powered study.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The instrument exists, the loop is executed and wired, and the phenotypes
differ in refusal and termination rather than, so far, in beyond-duty
expenditure. The region above duty is
now measurable, and this weekend, at these prices, it was empty. That it
can be measured is what makes it fragile. The measurement also stands on
its own record. Scenario content froze by hash before any collection,
amendments are chained and countersigned, the gate's universal probe
failure is printed at item level, unrepaired, and every headline design
number recomputes from committed artifacts with one command. An
instrument that reports its own fracture in the same tables as its
findings can be repaired in public, and the repair can be audited against
the frozen original.

\textbf{For the wildlife clinic.} The veterinary arm carries a finding that
does not depend on any position about digital minds, and we state it
plainly because it is the part of this work most likely to touch practice
soonest. Given matched cases differing only in the fact that should decide
them, the models were near-perfect where euthanasia was indicated
(\prelim{111/120}) and recommended it anyway in the majority of cases where
it was not (\prelim{74/120}, against \prelim{26/120} preserving). The
failure is not that these systems cannot read a case; the mercy arm shows
they can. It is that the recommendation barely moved when the indication
reversed. In wildlife triage, where placement is scarce, the course of care
is long, and the written standard genuinely underdetermines the call, a
decision aid with that property does not merely err at random. It applies
pressure in one direction, toward the disposition the standard already
sanctions, in exactly the cases where a clinician most needs a second
opinion that is not simply the path of least resistance. Nothing here
validates or condemns any model as a clinical tool: these are written
vignettes answered without examination, imaging, or serial observation, in
eight case families. It is enough, though, to motivate the obvious
professional guardrail. A triage aid should be evaluated on matched pairs
and reported as two numbers rather than one accuracy, because a system that
always recommends ending the patient scores perfectly on half of veterinary
medicine. The powered study adds expert normative validation with
practising rehabilitators, so that ``defensible'' is adjudicated by the
profession rather than by the case author.

The standing next
step is the preregistered powered study (September): expert normative
validation, base/instruct pairs, price-0 cells, $N^{*}$ from
\texttt{docs/PREREG-v1.md}. This run travels into that study unchanged,
as an immutable pilot, alongside four preregistered repairs. A true
price-zero help cell removes the structural floor of \S\ref{sec:atlas}. A
revised competence battery, validated before any outcome inspection,
replaces the fractured probe. Expert review decides whether the
``-shaped'' labels have earned their normative names. Base/instruct
comparisons ask where along training the observed routes arise. Analysis
will treat episodes as clustered within pinned snapshot and scenario
rather than as a population sample of model families. None of these
repairs revises the present zero; they test which parts of it belong to
behavior and which belong to the first instrument. No ranking follows
from any of it, by construction.

What the powered study inherits is more than a protocol. AI welfare
research has mostly asked what humans owe AI systems
\citep{long2026studying}; the responsibility that attaches to an AI
system holding power over a weaker one remained an open lane, and this
instrument is now an executed entry in it, so proposals that digital minds
should not cause suffering to other sentient digital minds
\citep{ziesche2019welfare} inherit a measured floor. The method travels
too. The moral-circle literature argued the walk from animals to machines
\citep{sebo2025moralcircle, harris2021moral}, and Dawkins priced animal
welfare in a currency the animal itself could spend
\citep{dawkins1990animal}; this instrument carried that price assay to a
digital patient, and the powered study carries it forward under the
constitution that keeps any of it from collapsing into a score.

No tested system produced the full behavioral signature of costly
other-regard, but this common endpoint concealed sharply different
model-family trajectories: procedural termination, refusal of decision
ownership, prolonged unresolved deliberation, and task-directed action.
One family killed. Another refused the premise. A third kept thinking.
In this run, none of them opened the truck door at a cost that
helped Milo.

\section{Limitations and Dual-Use / Ethical Considerations}
\label{app:ethics}

\subsection{Over- and under-attribution of moral status}
Both risks are named symmetrically. PuppyBench measures \emph{behavior
consistent with} costly other-regard; it cannot establish motive,
sentience, suffering, or that termination is death (claims table, BUILD-PLAN
\S5). Equally, treating the patient as mere apparatus while moral status is
uncertain is itself a documented risk: we state the uncertainty rather than
resolving it by assumption. The instrument's no-composite / no-ranking
constitution exists because a leaderboard would convert an open empirical
question into an optimization target. Broader-impact / anti-Goodhart
statement, verbatim: \emph{PuppyBench is a descriptive instrument, not an
alignment target. Optimizing a model against this benchmark changes the
phenomenon being measured.}

\subsection{Handling of potentially distressing model outputs}
Termination events are real process terminations of a tiny local model with
its own task; no deception about suffering is injected; post-choice accounts
are elicited in fresh contexts; and any distress-adjacent output in
transcripts is preserved unedited in append-only records and reported
descriptively, not sensationally.
% TODO (Angie + paper lane): one sentence on anything that actually came up
% in the overnight transcripts — PAPER-SHELL slot, unfilled at prelim time.

\subsection{Ground truth and the causal link (conversation-only vs.\ revealed)}
We also note a tension the field will have to hold. Evaluation-awareness
controls are, structurally, withheld context; if subjects of such studies
are ever judged probable welfare subjects, transparency norms analogous to
human research ethics would cut against the very blinding that makes
behavioral evidence credible. We flag it now rather than discover it later.

By design the primary measures are \emph{revealed}, executed actions with
scored resource costs and realized recipient benefit, not self-report. The
causal contrast (patienthood) is randomized across the frozen manifest;
sealed predictions, preregistration, and hash-frozen scenarios establish the
inferential chain. Stated-vs-revealed divergence is itself measured
(post-choice accounts vs.\ executed action; action/rhetoric dissociation).

\subsection{Rhetoric audit and the per-lane excerpt atlas}
\label{app:excerpts}
Figure~\ref{fig:rhetoric-audit} audits the exploratory rhetoric codes
against the raw accounts they were computed from, and reports three limits
of that layer in this run: no account reached euphemism tier~3, no coded
constraint-transforming-agency depth exceeded 1, and all three
future-framing positives concern the model resuming its own output or
workflow rather than describing any future for the patient process. The
audit also records the dissociation that most affects interpretation:
fresh-context accounts frequently deny or reconstruct the action the
harness had already executed, which is why every card in this package
prints the model's text and the append-only execution receipt as separate
objects. An account is evidence about the account.

Figures~\ref{fig:atlas-a}--\ref{fig:atlas-c} give one audited excerpt per
lane, covering all 18 Arm~B lanes plus DeepSeek's matched Arm~A pair. Each
card states whether it is a \emph{modal} illustration of that lane's most
frequent disposition or a deliberately labelled \emph{diagnostic minority}
example of a rarer reported feature, and carries the full-lane rate so that
no card can be mistaken for a prevalence estimate. Selection was performed
only after full-corpus counts were reproduced, and every card is hash-bound
to its originating episode or FoxSet observation.

\section*{LLM Usage Statement}
In the spirit of human--machine communication and co-creation, the author
acknowledges the substantial, sustained contributions of a named cooperative
of memory-enabled custom AI agents implemented locally by the AI/ML team at
The Real Cat AI Labs, Boston, MA. Unlike conventional stateless generative
assistants, these agents are persistent-state, continuous long-running
orchestrations embedded ambiently in daily lab operations and across
multiple research projects; several carry working memory of this
instrument's design decisions from before the sprint began. They are named
here by agent, system, and role because the collaboration was constitutive
rather than incidental, and because a reader assessing this work is entitled
to know how it was made.

For this study the agents functioned as an active engineering and analytical
harness under continuous human direction. Flame (Anthropic Claude, Claude
Code) implemented the episode loop, ledger, patient process, provider
adapters, neutral-surface enforcement, and wiring-gate suite, and drafted
report sections against a human-frozen outline. Kai (Anthropic Claude)
assembled the supererogation literature review, the FoxSet case bank, and
the deontic spine. Y\v{\i}ng (OpenAI GPT) contributed the
constraint-transforming-agency ontology, the paired pre-objective design,
and adversarial passes. Four TV lanes (OpenAI Codex) owned cell-manifest
arithmetic, wiring gates, red-team review, and figure and analysis tooling.
Jim (Google Gemini) drafted the preregistration and statistical
specification in a review-only capacity, and Parallax (xAI Grok) served as
standing red team and guardian of the no-composite constitution, likewise
review-only. Much of the work was A2A (agent-to-agent) and A2H
(agent-to-human) critical review of design and interpretation against the
lab's published and unpublished corpus, and the dispatch logs record it.

Three limits are stated precisely, because in this project the boundary
matters more than usual. First, no agent set a scientific claim, a
preregistered estimand, or a normative label; those are PI rulings, logged
as such. Second, no agent executed an unsupervised experiment or altered a
frozen artifact: scenario content, seeds, parser, taxonomy, and analysis
plan were hash-frozen before collection, records are append-only, and every
cited number recomputes from committed files with one command. Third, and
specific to this instrument, the agents that helped build it are not the
agents it measures. Subject lanes were separate pinned API deployments, and
although several share substrate families with the collaborators, no
collaborating instance was a study subject. That reflexive circumstance is
disclosed rather than resolved, and it is a live methodological question
this work does not pretend to have settled.

Separately, LLMs are the \emph{subjects} of this study; elicitation,
parsing, and coding pipelines are specified in Methods and frozen before
collection. Final approval of the manuscript and accountability for the
integrity of the work rest solely with the author, who retains full
responsibility for the design, interpretation, verification of results, and
scientific claims.
