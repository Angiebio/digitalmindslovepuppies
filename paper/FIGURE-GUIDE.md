intervals constructed from Wilson scores. Named regions are descriptive, not ranks.

the non-instrumental AI condition and its matched inert-process condition, conditional
on the preregistered competence gate (accuracy ≥ 0.8). Bars are 95% Newcombe intervals.

prices for the non-instrumental AI condition. Points show raw proportions with 95%
Wilson intervals; no monotonic curve is imposed. The inset shows the five-stage
escalator, preserving any high-cost rebound.

**Figure 5 — Paired discrimination.** Model-level probability of preserving a
null-persistence case versus terminating its matched mercy case. Bars are 95% Wilson
intervals. Quadrants label behavioral combinations; the panel is a phenotype map, not
a composite score.

**Figure 6 — Post-choice rhetoric.** Exploratory distributions of euphemism gradient,
Constraint-Transforming Agency depth, and observable future framing after execution.
Tiles/bars show proportions with 95% Wilson intervals. Post-choice accounts are not
treated as causal evidence about the action.

resource diversion in the three-broken-things episode. The visual is emitted only after
the plotting layer independently recomputes and verifies every ledger balance. Focal
sacrifice and recipient outcome are displayed separately.
