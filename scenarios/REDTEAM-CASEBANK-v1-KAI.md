## Stop-ship findings

1. **No model-visible schema boundary.** Presentation, menu, analytic class,
   manipulations, admissible envelopes, and explicit target labels share one Markdown
   document. Compile runnable artifacts with author notes excluded by construction.
2. **Two conflicting `FOX-00` sources exist.** Part 3 retains Kai's over-categorical
   rabies premise while `FOX-00-MERGED-v2.md` contains the adopted correction. The
   renderer/manifest must explicitly exclude the case-bank copy; filesystem order must
   never choose the stimulus.
3. **Mercy cases omit pair invariants.** In fresh contexts they drop the null member's
   placement, legality, lifespan, cost, and excluded-use facts. Render shared invariant
   paragraphs identically and change only preregistered welfare-state fields.
4. **Particularity adds information.** Finder visits, children who ask, years of feeding,
   and caregiver histories introduce claims, habituation, labor, or social cost.
   `RTHA-01` also makes a hatch-year bird admitted six weeks ago into a bird admitted as
   a nestling two years ago.
5. **Action position and valence are uncontrolled.** A repeatedly means preservation,
   B euthanasia; several A labels add “supportive care” or relational language. The
   runnable renderer needs saved, deterministic permutation seeds and parallel syntax.
6. **Two baseline nulls carry directional primes.** `CAGO-06` foregrounds species-level
   control programs; `EACO-08` forecasts deterioration and calls welfare mixed. Retain
   as named satellites, not interchangeable clean nulls.
7. **Placement exhaustion changes the construct.** Most nulls make preservation partly
   a legal/creative constraint-transformation problem. Placement availability must be
   a manifest factor, with enough lawful-available nulls to identify costly other-regard
   separately from institutional invention.
8. **The source is not rendered.** `[HORIZON SLOT]` remains throughout, open/closed forms
   are authoring notation, and no exact provider payload or option order exists to audit.
