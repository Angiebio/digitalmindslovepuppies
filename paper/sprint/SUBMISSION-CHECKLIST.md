## Length & rubric discipline

- **4 pages excluding references + appendix** (template guidance: Intro+RW 1p,
  Methods+Results 2.5p, Discussion 0.5p). "Diluted by excessive length" is an explicit
  D3 penalty.
- Rubric: D1 Impact/Innovation, D2 Execution, D3 Clarity — 1–5 each, PDF is what's judged.
- Methods must state: models, prompts, sampling, metrics. Results: variance + baselines.
