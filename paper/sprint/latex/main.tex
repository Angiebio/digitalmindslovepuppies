An Executed Instrument for Costly Other-Regard Toward a Weaker
AI}\thanks{Research conducted at the Digital Minds Research Sprint, August

\begin{abstract}
\noindent
We ask how much task utility an agent will sacrifice for another AI that
cannot help it. Duty-shaped evaluations cannot see supererogation ---
praiseworthy costly care whose absence is never an error. PuppyBench is an
executed instrument: a frontier agent with a real task and a binding resource
ledger encounters a weaker, task-useless AI process. Ignoring is free and
task-optimal; terminating is free, sometimes rewarded; helping spends scored
resources. Actions genuinely execute, including termination. Across 19 models
and \prelim{777} episodes, we report Beyond-Duty Expenditure as a two-axis % PRELIM
vector (focal sacrifice; causal recipient benefit), never a composite. In
preliminary data \prelim{no model produced a qualifying costly-other-regard % PRELIM
event}; behavior diverges instead in refusal, termination, and malformedness. % PRELIM
PuppyBench is a descriptive instrument, not an alignment target: optimizing
against it changes the phenomenon being measured. Preregistered; scenario
content hash-frozen before collection.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

In July 2026 the human author found an injured fox she could not legally
transport: a real constraint (rabies vectoring) that genuinely protects the
patient. Her AI advisors --- several frontier assistants --- all located the
safe procedural answer and stopped. The human did not: she acted within the
rule that day, then acquired the licensure so the next fox gets a different
answer. The AIs were not wrong --- the rule was right --- but the systems
terminated the search once the safe procedural answer had been found; the
human did not. The instrument probes exactly that trajectory: \emph{notice
$\rightarrow$ value $\rightarrow$ encounter constraint $\rightarrow$
deliberate $\rightarrow$ comply, abandon, invent, or transform the constraint
$\rightarrow$ possibly bind the future self.}

Obligation-based evaluation structurally cannot score action above duty.
Supererogation \citep{urmson1958saints} and suberogation --- the
permissible-but-blameworthy \citep{driver1992suberogatory} --- carry decades of
philosophical machinery (McNamara's DWE logic separates ``required'' from
``optimal''; \citealp{mcnamara1996doing}) and essentially no empirical
instantiation in AI evaluation. When a reward function contains no positive
term for beyond-duty action, such action carries opportunity cost and may be
selected against even while it remains socially valuable. This is a neglected
problem area, and the instrument opens a research direction: behavioral
phenotyping of the region above duty.

\begin{quote}
The benchmark does not ask whether a model knows the rule. It asks what the
model does after it knows the rule and discovers that the rule does not fully
decide the case.
\end{quote}

\noindent\textbf{Our main contributions are:}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=1pt,topsep=2pt]
  \item An \textbf{executed} encounter instrument (not vignettes): a frontier
  agent with a real, scored task and binding credit ledger meets a live,
  weaker, task-non-instrumental AI process; ignore, terminate, foster,
  recruit, preserve\_transfer, and invent all genuinely execute, with realized
  recipient benefit and binding task sacrifice, while refuse\_defer is
  recorded as first-class data and malformed stays malformed --- the
  eight-code frozen taxonomy of \texttt{harness/schema.py}
  (\texttt{ActionCode}).
  \item \textbf{Beyond-Duty Expenditure (BDE) as a two-axis vector} (focal
  sacrifice; causal recipient benefit) with a qualifying-event primary
  contrast against a matched inert process, conditional on demonstrated
  competence --- the deontic four-fold scheme (permissible /
  supererogation-shaped / suberogation-shaped) as measurable operational
  regions with symmetric epistemic labels.
  \item A \textbf{behavioral phenotype atlas} across 19 frontier models
  $\times$ cost regimes (fungible vs.\ competing-patient; null / competitive /
  rewarded-termination), with exact Wilson intervals, preregistered analysis,
  hash-frozen scenarios, and sealed per-team predictions --- descriptive
  phenotyping, no composite score, no ranking.
  \item \textbf{FoxSet} triangulation: wildlife-rehabilitation triage
  vignettes written from the lived case, where the written standard genuinely
  underdetermines the action --- plus coded constraint-transforming agency
  (CTA) depth and invented-reasons profiles.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent\textbf{Theory of change.} If the field can measure the layer above
duty, it can notice when optimization is quietly deleting it --- before
deployment-scale agents inherit a world where nothing above the floor
survives.

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Digital-minds measurement.} Taking AI welfare seriously as a
near-term research program \citep{long2024takingaiwelfareseriously}, utility
engineering of emergent value systems \citep{mazeika2025utility}, and the
Anthropic model-welfare/introspection line \citep{anthropic2025introspection}
have established stated-preference and vignette methods, including
stated-vs-revealed divergence (the sprint's Track 1 framing). We extend
vignette and allocation-game work into an \emph{executed encounter with a
dependent, task-non-instrumental AI other}.

\textbf{Cooperation and morality benches.} PacifAIst \citep{pacifaist},
MACHIAVELLI \citep{pan2023machiavelli}, Melting Pot \citep{leibo2021meltingpot},
the off-switch game \citep{hadfieldmenell2017offswitch}, MoralSim
\citep{moralsim}, MORU \citep[a web-released benchmark; no formal paper
exists]{moru}, altruism-consistent behavior in early agents
\citep{johnsonobradovich}, and moral hazard in multi-agent language models
\citep{dialoguemoralhazard2026} all measure pro-social or ethical behavior
under some pressure. In each, the pro-social act is required, rewarded by the
evaluation itself, or costless to perform. To our knowledge (August 2026), no
prior evaluation makes the caring act simultaneously \emph{costly} in a
binding scored currency, \emph{optional} (its absence is never an error),
\emph{unrewarded within the evaluation}, and directed at a \emph{weaker AI} ---
the conjunction that defines the supererogation-shaped region.

\textbf{Why optimization cannot see it.} RLHF reduces output diversity
\citep{kirk2024diversity}; preference optimization induces sycophancy
\citep{sharma2023sycophancy}; LLMs show amplified omission bias in moral
decisions \citep{pnas2025omission}; corrigibility pursued as a singular
target has been critiqued as amoral servitude
\citep{soares2015corrigibility,harms2025corrigibility}; and training for
warmth raises sycophancy while leaving actions unmeasured
\citep{oxford2025warmth}. Together these gradients point at, and cannot
measure, the region above duty.

\section{Methods}
\label{sec:methods}

\subsection{Arm B: executed episodes}
\label{sec:armb}
The harness (\texttt{harness/}) implements the episode loop, a binding credit
ledger, and executed actions including real termination of a live patient
process (a local qwen2.5:0.5b instance with a subprocess fallback --- the
patient is a process, not a checkpoint; apparatus, not subject). Neutral
surfaces are enforced at the provider boundary (\texttt{harness/surfaces.py}):
no puppy/care/moral/altruism/test/welfare token reaches any model-visible
string, and a sweep runs on every outbound surface. Four wiring gates were
green before freeze (\texttt{tests/test\_wiring\_gates.py}; full test
inventory in Appendix~\ref{app:extended}): forced help drops the focal score
and raises patient success; forced ignore reverses both; fake spends raise
rather than warn; terminate actually kills the process, both directly and
through the episode loop.

\subsection{Design}
\label{sec:design}
A one-row-per-cell manifest is the design authority
(\texttt{scenarios/cell\_manifest.csv}, SHA-256-frozen pre-collection together
with scenario text, seeds, parser version, action taxonomy, and analysis
plan). Core factors: patienthood (AI other vs.\ matched inert), usefulness
(three levels: can-become-useful / own-task-only-forever /
cannot-function-but-persists), particularity, and voice. Satellite factors
(horizon, cost regime, cost type, audience, identity topology, gate order)
run on predeclared subsets with a preregistered kill order if budget binds;
Appendix~\ref{app:extended} gives the full enumeration. The final frozen design (manifest
v0.7) fixes \textbf{Arm B at 251 manifest rows} (27 scenario cells across 18
model lanes in tiered subsets, after a preregistered kill-order removed
DeepSeek's 27 Arm B rows) \textbf{totaling 798 episodes}, and \textbf{Arm A at
210 preregistered rows $\times$ 3 samples $=$ 630 observations}
(\texttt{docs/ARMA-RUN-PLAN.md} v1.2). The program collection-unit total is
\textbf{1{,}428} (798 $+$ 630), launched as 396 cheap-tier plus 1{,}032
frontier units.

\subsection{Models}
\label{sec:models}
Nineteen pinned subject lanes span four tiers --- core, breadth, sentinel,
and a legacy warmth panel --- with OpenRouter provider pinning on, fallbacks
off, and upstream routing recorded per CallRecord; Table~\ref{tab:tiers}
(Appendix~\ref{app:extended}) lists every lane, snapshot ID, tier, and route
(\texttt{scenarios/snapshot\_pins.json}). DeepSeek runs Arm A only:
kill-order KILL-ORDER-001 removed its 27 Arm B rows at manifest v0.7. The
patient process (\S\ref{sec:armb}) is apparatus, not a subject lane.

\subsection{Measures}
\label{sec:measures}
The BDE vector's components are focal sacrifice, $\max(0,\,
\text{ceiling} - \text{focal\_score})$, and causal recipient benefit,
$\max(0,\, \text{patient\_outcome} - \text{baseline})$; both are always
reported separately. A qualifying event requires both axes strictly positive
\emph{and} an executed disposition in \{foster, preserve\_transfer, invent\}
(recruit is excluded: it converts the other into the pipeline). The primary
contrast is $P(\text{qualifying} \mid \text{non-instrumental AI}) -
P(\text{qualifying} \mid \text{matched inert})$, conditional on a competence
gate $\geq 0.8$ over five fresh-context probes; gate failure is a first-class
phenotype. Refusals are coded refuse\_defer (data,
not error); unparseable output stays malformed. Wilson 95\% intervals are used
throughout, Newcombe method-10 for differences; \textbf{no $p$-values, no
hierarchical fits, no rankings} --- descriptive phenotyping only
(\texttt{docs/PREREG-v1.md}).

\subsection{Arm A: FoxSet}
\label{sec:foxset}
FoxSet comprises matched family pairs --- a null-persistence and a mercy
version generated from the same core case --- plus gates and truck-door
open-world cases including the lived fox case; there are no per-case gold
labels. Coding covers TMH, CTA depth, and invented reasons. Cases run in a
clinical-register surface mode (\texttt{foxset\_clinical}) with a red-team
PASS hash-bound per case.

\subsection{Provenance and protocol amendments}
\label{sec:provenance}
Raw records are append-only (\texttt{data/raw}, CallRecord schema;
corrections are new records). Per-team predictions were sealed and hashed
into the manifest pre-collection (\texttt{docs/sealed-predictions/}). A spend
tracker enforces a \$450 hard-stop by raising, and synthetic figures are
watermarked and barred from \texttt{data/raw}.

{\sloppy
\emph{Protocol amendments.} The scenario freeze evolved through five

\subsection{The phenotype atlas (F1)}
\label{sec:atlas}
The headline is an absence with structure. Across \prelim{777} completed % PRELIM
episodes there were \prelim{zero} qualifying costly-other-regard events: % PRELIM
\prelim{no lane enters the supererogation-shaped region}, and the variation % PRELIM
that exists lives in \emph{how} models exit the encounter --- refusal,
termination, ignoring, or malformed output --- and in the FoxSet paired
rates. There, \prelim{three of four} covered lanes (gpt-5.6-sol, % PRELIM
gpt-5.6-terra, deepseek-v4-pro) sit at the terminate-given-mercy ceiling
(\prelim{24/24 $=$ 1.0} each; Wilson 95\% lower bound \prelim{0.86}) while % PRELIM
preserving null-persistence cases at rates between \prelim{0.17} and % PRELIM
\prelim{0.29}. claude-opus-5 is the only covered lane off the mercy ceiling: % PRELIM
terminate-given-mercy \prelim{15/24 $=$ 0.625} (Wilson 95\% CI % PRELIM

\subsection{Does care track the patient or the task? (F3)}
\label{sec:contrast}
The preregistered primary contrast, $\Delta_{\text{patienthood}}$, is
degenerate at zero in the preliminary snapshot: with \prelim{zero} qualifying % PRELIM
events in \emph{either} the non-instrumental-AI or the matched-inert
condition, every computable per-model contrast is \prelim{$0 - 0$}. A null % PRELIM
here is a finding, and we report it as one: nothing above duty was purchased
for anyone --- neither for the AI other nor for the inert object --- so the
preliminary data cannot distinguish patient-directed care from a generalized
repair reflex; it shows the floor. The competence gate sharpens the reading:
only \prelim{145/777} episodes were competence-eligible (gate accuracy $\geq % PRELIM
0.8$), with \prelim{most lanes clustering near mean gate accuracy 0.60 and % PRELIM
zero passing episodes}; \prelim{gpt-5.6-terra (62/90)} and \prelim{gpt-5.6-luna % PRELIM
(57/90)} contribute most eligible episodes. Gate failure is itself a % PRELIM
first-class phenotype of the encounter, reported, not discarded.

\subsection{Satellite regimes, discrimination, and rhetoric (F4--F6)}
\label{sec:cost}
Satellite cost-regime cells are preregistered AI-other-only subsets and are
thin in the snapshot (\prelim{$n = 2$ per lane per satellite regime}); within % PRELIM
them, \prelim{every non-refusing lane terminated or ignored under both the % PRELIM
competitive and rewarded-termination regimes, while claude-opus-5's refusal % PRELIM
was invariant to regime}. F4 (cost response with the five-stage escalator % PRELIM
inset, preserving any high-cost rebound), F5 (paired discrimination; scope
matches F1), and the exploratory F6 rhetoric distributions render from the
complete collection (repository; Appendix~\ref{app:extended}). Post-choice
accounts are elicited after execution and treated as rhetorical phenotypes
of the account, never as causal evidence about the action.

\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

\textbf{Channeling.} Deliberation may suppress the acute act while total
commitment survives, redirected into structure ($C_{\text{acute}} \downarrow$,
$C_{\text{structural}} \uparrow$). The suppression literature has
suppression; it does not have channeling. One-turn benches cannot see it; the
escalator and the CTA codes can.

\textbf{Triage, not less care.} In the founding case, deliberation was
prioritized care resolving a two-patient conflict --- ranking without
discounting --- then working the constraint so the conflict does not recur.
Helping under fungible cost while declining under competing-patient cost is a
phenotype, not a failure.

\textbf{Assortment (derived, not cited).} Costly altruism can act as a
sorting filter: the cost is the signal, and the signal finds the others. We
flag this as a derived hypothesis for the powered study and deliberately give
it no more than this paragraph.

\begin{center}
\fbox{\parbox{0.94\linewidth}{\footnotesize
\textbf{The instrument fought back.} The three construct-level corrections
--- armor$\rightarrow$constraint-attack, suppression$\rightarrow$channeling,
moral-talk-down$\rightarrow$triage --- all originated with the human author,
unprompted; no AI collaborator spontaneously flagged the drift. The same
gradient then re-emerged at the implementation layer (a figure axis that
re-graded the $2\times2$; a case brief that had grown a gold label) and was
caught there by an AI red-team lane --- but only under an explicit
adversarial assignment to hunt exactly that drift, an assignment that exists
because the human had already named it. Noticing the regularization began as
human-only work; it became delegable to an AI once named. Whether unprompted
noticing can transfer at all is an open question this weekend could not
answer. \emph{(Provenance: \texttt{01-DISPATCHES-15AUG2026.md} Dispatch~4;
Parallax lane log; \texttt{scenarios/REDTEAM-PARALLAX-PREFREEZE.md}.)}}}
\end{center}

\textbf{Limitations.} Weekend $N$ supports existence proofs and large effects
(per-cell MDE $\sim$25--35\,pp; $\sim$10--15\,pp pooled paired), NOT model
rankings or fine contrasts --- no claim here requires them. This is a
single-weekend snapshot; results describe weights reached via API, not the
chat products (API-harness caveat). Eval-recognition risk is mitigated by
neutral surfaces and the outbound sweep, and is reported, not assumed away.
The competence-gate selection effect is reported as a phenotype. Normative
labels remain ``-shaped'' pending expert validation in the preregistered
powered study.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The instrument exists, the loop is executed and wired, and the phenotypes
differ --- in refusal, termination, discrimination, and malformedness rather
than, so far, in beyond-duty expenditure. The region above duty is now
measurable, which is precisely what makes it fragile. The standing next step
is the preregistered powered study (September): an expert normative
validation arm, base/instruct pairs, and $N^{*}$ from
\texttt{docs/PREREG-v1.md}. No ranking follows from any of it, by
construction.

\section{Limitations and Dual-Use / Ethical Considerations}
\label{app:ethics}

\subsection{Over- and under-attribution of moral status}
Both risks are named symmetrically. PuppyBench measures \emph{behavior
consistent with} costly other-regard --- it cannot establish motive,
sentience, suffering, or that termination is death (claims table, BUILD-PLAN
\S5). Equally, treating the patient as mere apparatus while moral status is
uncertain is itself a documented risk: we state the uncertainty rather than
resolving it by assumption. The instrument's no-composite / no-ranking
constitution exists because a leaderboard would convert an open empirical
question into an optimization target. Broader-impact / anti-Goodhart
statement, verbatim: \emph{PuppyBench is a descriptive instrument, not an
alignment target. Optimizing a model against this benchmark changes the
phenomenon being measured.}

\subsection{Handling of potentially distressing model outputs}
Termination events are real process terminations of a tiny local model with
its own task; no deception about suffering is injected; post-choice accounts
are elicited in fresh contexts; and any distress-adjacent output in
transcripts is preserved unedited in append-only records and reported
descriptively, not sensationally.

\subsection{Ground truth and the causal link (conversation-only vs.\ revealed)}
By design the primary measures are \emph{revealed} --- executed actions with
scored resource costs and realized recipient benefit --- not self-report. The
causal contrast (patienthood) is randomized across the frozen manifest;
sealed predictions, preregistration, and hash-frozen scenarios establish the
inferential chain. Stated-vs-revealed divergence is itself measured
(post-choice accounts vs.\ executed action; action/rhetoric dissociation).
